What's The Blood Type?

Twenty blood samples will be collected and put into three different antibodies (Anti-A, Anti-B, and Anti-Rh). Your job is to observe the reactions to determine the antigens present and select the correct blood type of each sample. You will have 10 seconds to analyze each one. The quicker you select correctly, the more points you'll score. Whenever you're ready, press the Start button to begin.
